About Upaadhyay

One platform connecting faculty and institutions

Upaadhyay is a faculty recruitment platform built for Indian education. It connects schools, junior colleges and universities directly with qualified teaching talent — one profile, every opportunity, with no middlemen between the faculty who teach and the institutions that hire them.

Built around how Indian education actually hires

Every listing and every profile is sorted into the category it belongs to, so faculty are matched with roles suited to their level.
